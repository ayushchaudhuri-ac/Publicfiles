Microsoft Copilot
Your Microsoft 365 layer
Emails Files Teams SharePoint
What it does
Finds things across your Microsoft estate using plain language — emails, documents, Teams messages, and shared drives.
When to use
Best when the answer lives inside your Microsoft tools. Works across the whole org, not just your own inbox.
Example
"Find all emails from Lee Bond about overtime costs" or "Locate the SSB capacity model in SharePoint"
ChatGPT
Working through hard problems
Problem-solving Frameworks Reasoning
What it does
Multi-step reasoning and detailed instruction-following. Holds logic and structure well across a long, multi-turn conversation.
When to use
Give it rich context and push with follow-ons. Best for structured thinking — not quick lookups.
Example
"How do I build a value framework across cost, quality & revenue without double-counting?"
Claude
Build & create things
Dashboards Documents Anticipates needs
What it does
Builds structured outputs — dashboards, trackers, decks, documents — and intuitively works ahead without being explicitly asked.
When to use
Strong on professional writing, code, and structured deliverables. Picks up on context and works ahead of you.
Example
"Build me a pipeline dashboard from this data" — it will suggest KPIs, layout & filters unprompted
Gemini + NotebookLM
Images & doc-grounded AI
Image gen Presentations Docs-only LLM
What it does
Gemini leads on image creation. NotebookLM acts as an AI only on the documents you upload — preventing hallucination by design.
Key callout
NotebookLM = a small language model on your own documents. Also generates quick presentations from those same docs.
Example
Upload your project docs — ask questions, get answers grounded only in those sources. Zero hallucination risk.
AlphaSense
Expert & institutional research
Expert transcripts Earnings calls Cited sources
What it does
Premium access to expert calls, analyst transcripts, broker notes & regulatory filings. Every answer links to a verified primary source.
When to use
Use when you need to know what executives, analysts, or regulators have said — with full citations.
Example
"What are water utility CEOs saying about AMP8 investment priorities?" — with full transcript quotes
Perplexity
Cited real-time web research
Real-time web Cited links Source-first
What it does
Searches the live web and surfaces cited, linked sources alongside every answer. Far more trustworthy than a basic Google search.
When to use
Use when accuracy and source credibility matter. Neither it nor AlphaSense will fabricate a reference.
Example
"What has Ofwat said about ODIs in the last 6 months?" — with direct links to primary sources

How to use it

Three steps.
That’s it.

Step 01
Search for what you need
Type anything — a task, a tool, an outcome. Search runs across titles, descriptions and the full prompt text, so you’ll find it even if you don’t know what it’s called.
Try: "email", "AlphaSense", "competitor", "JSON"
Step 02
Filter by topic
Use the topic chips to narrow to Built by us, Research, Creative, Development and more. Combine with search to find what sits at the intersection.
Or click any topic tile above to jump straight there.
Step 03
Open, read, copy
Click any card for the full write-up — summary at the top, what it is, when to use, complete prompt. One button copies the prompt to your clipboard.
Feeling lucky? Hit Surprise me for a random pick.
